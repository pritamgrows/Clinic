Why Clinics Stall

Revenue leaks at 3 predictable points.

Most clinics have no patient acquisition system. So they lose patients they already paid to attract.

A patient searches for a clinic, finds yours, and fills out a contact form.
→No instant reply. They move to the next clinic down the list. You paid for that click. Your competitor got the patient.
A patient books a consultation or OPD appointment.
→No reminder goes out. They forget or deprioritise. The slot goes empty. That's ₹500–₹5,000 lost per empty consultation slot.
A patient visits, gets treated, and leaves satisfied.
→Six months pass. No follow-up. They go to a different clinic for their next visit. Lifetime value: gone.

None of this is your team's fault. It's a systems problem — and it has a straightforward fix.
